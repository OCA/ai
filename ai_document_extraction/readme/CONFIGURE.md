1. Create or configure an *AI Connection* under *Settings > Technical > AI > AI
   Connections* using your preferred vision-capable AI connection provider.
2. Link the AI connection either:
   - Per journal: in *Accounting > Configuration > Journals*, open a purchase or
     sales journal and set **AI Document Extraction Connection** under the
     *Advanced Settings* tab.
   - Company-wide: in *Accounting > Configuration > Settings*, under the **AI
     Document Extraction** section, select the default **AI Connection** and
     adjust the **Partner Match Threshold** (default: `85`).
