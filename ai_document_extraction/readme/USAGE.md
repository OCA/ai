1. Go to *Accounting > Vendors > Bills* (or *Customers > Invoices*).
2. Upload a single-page or multi-page PDF or image invoice, or attach it to a
   draft invoice and click **Extract with AI**.
3. When extraction finishes, *AI Extraction State* becomes **Done** and the
   partner, invoice date, reference, currency, and invoice lines are populated.
4. If the partner could not be matched automatically, click **Review
   Extraction** to select the partner and apply it to the draft move.
