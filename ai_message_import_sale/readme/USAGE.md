- The quotations created appear in *Message Import > Received > Sales
  quotations*, and in the documents, the senders and the receiving channels
  they come from. The card of a channel in the panel shows the ones to
  confirm.
- Each quotation shows where it comes from and its open incidents, and its
  internal note explains every decision.
- A correction received later updates the quotation while it can be modified;
  a value changed by a person is kept, with an incident.
