This module adds the document type *Customer order (sale)* to *AI
Message Import* (``ai_message_import``): the customer orders found in
the received conversations become draft sales quotations
(``sale.order``).

- **Customer**: the contact that is already a customer is preferred.
  When it does not exist, it is created only with reliable data: a name,
  enough confidence and a VAT, email, phone or address. Otherwise an
  incident keeps the quotation until a person chooses the customer.
- **Products**: never created. A product that is not identified leaves
  its line pending with an incident; the other lines make the quotation.
- **Price**: the pricelist of the customer, or the sales price of the
  product, with the standard computation of Odoo. The price of the
  document is only compared. A pricelist in another currency is not
  used, with an incident.
- **Units**: the unit of the product and its sales packagings.
- **Delivery**: the date of the order goes to the *Delivery Date*. The
  address is searched among the delivery addresses of the customer;
  addresses are never created.
- **Reference and payment terms**: the number of the order goes to the
  *Customer Reference*; the payment terms of the customer are kept
  unless the order gives other equivalent ones.
- **Confirmation**: never automatic. With open incidents, *Confirm*
  warns before confirming.

Configuration
=============

In *Message Import > Configuration > Settings*, *Create customers* and
its minimum confidence tell whether a customer that does not exist is
created from the order.

In *Message Import > Configuration > AI prompts*, write the prompt
*Document type: customer order (sale)*, which tells the AI when a
document is a customer order and who its customer is.

Usage
=====

- The quotations created appear in *Message Import > Received > Sales
  quotations*, and in the documents, the senders and the receiving
  channels they come from. The card of a channel in the panel shows the
  ones to confirm.
- Each quotation shows where it comes from and its open incidents, and
  its internal note explains every decision.
- A correction received later updates the quotation while it can be
  modified; a value changed by a person is kept, with an incident.
