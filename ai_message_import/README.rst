This module reads with an AI the conversations received by *Mail Message
Import* (``mail_message_import``) and turns the commercial documents
they bring (vendor quotes, customer orders...) into draft orders.

The AI only interprets: Odoo validates every answer and decides. Nothing
coming from the AI is used before it is validated, the prices of Odoo
prevail, and when in doubt an incident is created instead of guessing.

- The **AI connection** of the company (``ai_connection``, OCA/ai) reads
  the whole conversation: the texts, the PDF and the images, and the
  Word, Excel, CSV and forwarded emails turned into text. It tells the
  documents of the installed types and the corrections of the previous
  ones.
- Each **interpreted document** keeps what the AI read: the partner, the
  number, the date, the lines, the payment terms, the totals... The
  module of its type (purchases, sales...) creates or updates its draft
  order.
- The partner, the products and the units are found by exact rules (VAT,
  references, barcodes, names, learned equivalences). The lines no rule
  identifies can be chosen by the AI among the most similar products.
- What cannot be decided safely becomes an **incident**: an unknown
  partner or product, a price or total difference, a possible
  duplicate... The incidents of a partner, a product or a unit are
  solved by choosing the record and pressing **Apply and learn**: the
  choice is learned for the next documents written the same way.
- The **history of changes** keeps every value an update of the AI
  changed on an order, and a value changed by a person is never
  overwritten.

This module knows no document type: install at least one, as
``ai_message_import_purchase`` or ``ai_message_import_sale``, and a
channel of ``mail_message_import``, as ``mail_message_import_email`` or
``mail_message_import_whatsapp``.

Configuration
=============

1. Create an AI connection in *Settings > Technical > AI > AI
   Connection* (administrators only), for example with
   ``ai_connection_openai``. Its model must read PDF and images. The
   connection keeps the key of the provider.
2. In *Message Import > Configuration > Settings*, choose the *AI
   connection* of the company and review the other settings:

   - the days of previous documents sent to the AI to recognise
     corrections;
   - the maximum size of the files of a message and of a conversation;
   - the transport product, used for the transport charges of the
     documents;
   - whether the AI chooses the products no rule identifies, and the
     minimum confidence of its choice;
   - the difference allowed between the totals of a document and of its
     order.

3. In *Message Import > Configuration > AI prompts* (administrators
   only), write the prompts the installed modules need: the reading of
   the conversations, the choice of the products and one description per
   document type. They are created without text: until they are written,
   the settings warn which ones are missing and the conversations are
   left in error.
4. Give the users that review the documents the group *Message Import
   Manager* and the rights of the orders of each type.

Usage
=====

- *Message Import > Panel* shows, for each receiving channel, the
  documents to review, the new ones and the incidents.
- *Message Import > To review > Documents to review* lists the documents
  that are pending, possible duplicates or with open incidents; *New
  documents*, the ones you have not opened yet.
- *Message Import > To review > Incidents* tells what happened and how
  to solve it. For a partner, a product or a unit, choose the record and
  press **Apply and learn**: the document is applied again, and the open
  incidents written the same way are solved with it. Once the cause of
  any other incident is corrected, press **Retry**.
- An order with open incidents warns about them, and asks for a
  confirmation before it is confirmed.
- *Message Import > Configuration > What the AI learned* lists the
  products, contacts and units learned, which can also be added by hand.

For developers, a module of a document type:

- adds its type to ``ai.message.import.document._document_types()``,
  with its label and the builder of its orders, a subclass of
  ``services.order_builder.OrderBuilder``;
- creates the prompt that describes it, with the code
  ``document_type.<type>``;
- adds ``ai.message.import.order.mixin`` to its orders and
  ``ai.message.import.order.line.mixin`` to their lines.

A module that keeps a balance of AI tokens overrides
``mail.message.import.conversation._add_ai_tokens()``.
