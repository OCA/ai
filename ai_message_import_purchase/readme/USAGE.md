- The requests for quotation created appear in *Message Import > Received >
  Purchase orders*, and in the documents, the senders and the receiving
  channels they come from. The card of a channel in the panel shows the ones
  to confirm.
- Each request for quotation shows where it comes from and its open incidents,
  and its internal note explains every decision (prices, conversions, dates,
  payment terms...).
- A correction received later updates the request for quotation while it can
  be modified; a value changed by a person is kept, with an incident.
