This module adds the document type *Vendor quote (purchase)* to *AI
Message Import* (``ai_message_import``): the vendor quotes found in the
received conversations become draft requests for quotation
(``purchase.order``).

- **Vendor**: the contact that is already a vendor is preferred. When it
  does not exist, it is created if the AI identified it with enough
  confidence and a reliable data (VAT, email, phone or address).
- **Products**: besides the common rules, they are searched by the
  reference and the name in the pricelists of the vendor. A missing
  product is created when its line is complete (description, price,
  known unit, enough confidence), with the price of the document as
  cost: a service when the AI marks the line as such or its unit is of
  time, otherwise a consumable.
- **Price**: the pricelist of the vendor for the quantity, the unit, the
  currency and the dates; otherwise the cost of the product. The price
  of the document is only compared, and the difference is noted.
- **Units**: the purchase unit of the product and its purchase
  packagings.
- **Dates and delivery**: the date of the document or of each line,
  otherwise the one Odoo computes with the lead time of the vendor. The
  warehouse of the company is searched by its name or address; addresses
  and locations are never created.
- **Reference**: the number of the quote goes to the *Vendor Reference*.
- **Confirmation**: never automatic. With open incidents, *Confirm
  Order* warns before confirming.

Configuration
=============

In *Message Import > Configuration > Settings*:

- *Create vendors* and its minimum confidence: whether a vendor that
  does not exist is created from the document.
- *Create products (purchases)* and its minimum confidence: whether a
  product that does not exist is created from a complete line.

In *Message Import > Configuration > AI prompts*, write the prompt
*Document type: vendor quote (purchase)*, which tells the AI when a
document is a vendor quote and who its vendor is.

Usage
=====

- The requests for quotation created appear in *Message Import >
  Received > Purchase orders*, and in the documents, the senders and the
  receiving channels they come from. The card of a channel in the panel
  shows the ones to confirm.
- Each request for quotation shows where it comes from and its open
  incidents, and its internal note explains every decision (prices,
  conversions, dates, payment terms...).
- A correction received later updates the request for quotation while it
  can be modified; a value changed by a person is kept, with an
  incident.
